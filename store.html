03 / LAPTOP GUIDE

Which laptop
do I need?

Use this as a rough guide. Before you buy, we'll confirm the right specifications for your software and budget.

If your team mainly…Look forTypical roles
Uses email, documents, and the webCore i3 / Ryzen 3 or better · 8GB RAM · 256GB SSDAdmin, front desk, customer service
Works in large spreadsheets, accounting software, or many apps at onceCore i5 / Ryzen 5 · 16GB RAM · 512GB SSDAccounts, managers, operations
Designs, edits video, or uses CADCore i7 / Ryzen 7 · 16–32GB RAM · dedicated graphics · 512GB+ SSDDesigners, engineers, media
Writes code or works with dataCore i5–i7 / Ryzen 5–7 · 16GB+ RAM · 512GB SSDDevelopers, analysts
Serves customers at a counterDesktop or all-in-one · 8GB RAM · SSD · receipt printerReception, cashiers, point of sale

Always choose an SSD. It makes the biggest difference to everyday speed.

Plan for power cuts. Battery life matters for laptops, and desktops need a UPS.

Buying several? Choose one model for everyone so setup and support stay simple.
